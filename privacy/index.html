一句講晒:MathApp 係一個小朋友用嘅數學練習 app。所有學習紀錄預設只留喺你部機。我哋唔賣廣告、唔做追蹤、冇裝任何第三方分析 SDK、唔會賣你嘅資料。只有兩樣嘢會離開部機,而兩樣都由家長控制:①匿名診斷(預設關 —— 家長入去撳「我同意」先會開)同②每日 email 學習報告(要家長自己填 email 先會開)。

1. 邊個營運呢個 app

MathApp 由一位獨立開發者製作同營運,係一個為香港小學課程(小一至小六)設計嘅數學練習 app,喺 iPhone 同 iPad 上運行。本政策講明我哋點處理你同小朋友嘅資料。

如果你對本政策有任何疑問,請經支援頁聯絡我哋。

2. 留喺部機、唔會離開嘅資料

以下資料只儲存喺你部裝置入面,唔會上傳去任何伺服器,我哋亦睇唔到:

  • 小朋友嘅名同年級(由家長自己輸入,可以用花名)
  • 所有答題紀錄:做過邊題、答咗咩、啱定錯、用咗幾耐、用過幾多次提示、難度等級
  • 手寫草稿圖(保留 3 日後自動刪走)
  • 家長設定同 4 位數字家長密碼(PIN)
  • 遊戲進度(寵物、領地等)

MathApp 唔使開戶口、唔使登入、冇雲端同步。你喺 app 入面刪除一個小朋友檔案,或者刪除成個 app,呢啲資料就會喺部機上永久消失,冇任何備份喺我哋度。

3. 會離開部機嘅資料(共兩條通道)

3.1 匿名診斷(預設關,要家長明確開啟)

為咗知道 app 有冇喺小朋友部機度死機、載唔到題目、或者卡住,app 可以傳出匿名嘅技術紀錄。

呢項預設係關嘅。喺家長入「家長端 → 帳戶 → 匿名診斷 → 我同意」之前,app 一件事都唔會記低,亦都唔會傳任何嘢出去 —— 連上面講嘅安裝編號都唔會產生。

會傳唔會傳
• 一個隨機產生嘅安裝編號(UUID,同你嘅身分冇任何關係;刪 app 就永久消失)
• app 版本、iOS 版本、機種代號(例如 iPad13,1)
• 事件紀錄:開機、閃退、死機、畫面卡頓、題庫載入失敗、題庫更新結果、完成一組題(組數同首次答對率) 		• 小朋友個名
• 家長 email
• 題目內容同答案本身
• IDFA / IDFV 或任何廣告識別碼
• 位置、通訊錄、相片、麥克風、相機(app 完全冇要求過呢啲權限)

點開 / 點熄:app 內「家長端 → 帳戶 → 匿名診斷」。未表過態就會見到「我同意,開啟匿名診斷」同「唔要,多謝」兩個選擇;開咗之後隨時可以撳「關」。熄咗即刻停止記錄,連本機仲未傳出嘅紀錄都會即刻清走。

呢啲紀錄經 Google Apps Script 傳去我哋自己嘅一個 Google Sheet,只用嚟修 bug 同改善 app,唔會用嚟做廣告、唔會同任何人分享。

3.2 每日 email 學習報告(預設關,家長自己開)

家長可以喺「家長端 → 帳戶 → 每日 email 報告」填自己嘅 email。填咗之後,如果小朋友當日有做題,app 每日會寄一份摘要去你自己填嗰個信箱。

  • 報告內容:小朋友嘅名、年級、當日題數、正確率、連續日數、最弱課題、當日最多 5 條錯題(連小朋友答咗咩同正確答案)、近 7 日對比。
  • 條路點行:app → 我哋喺 Google Apps Script 上嘅一個小程式 → 用 Gmail 寄去你個 email。
  • 會唔會存起:唔會。個程式收到之後即刻寄信,唔會將你嘅 email 或者報告內容寫入任何資料庫。唯一嘅例外係一個防濫發嘅計數器,佢會喺記憶體暫存你個 email 最多 6 個鐘,之後自動消失。
  • 唔想要:喺 app 入面清空個 email 欄位,即刻停,冇任何嘢殘留。

3.3 題庫更新(冇個人資料)

App 會不時去 Cloudflare 嘅檔案儲存服務下載更新版題庫。呢個係一般網頁下載,同下載一個檔案冇分別 —— 唔會傳出任何關於你或小朋友嘅資料。同所有上網行為一樣,對方伺服器會喺技術紀錄見到你嘅 IP 地址。

3.4 訂閱同付款

如果你訂閱付費功能,交易完全由 Apple 嘅 App 內購買處理。我哋永遠見唔到你嘅信用卡號碼、Apple ID 或者付款資料。我哋只會由 Apple 收到一個「呢部裝置有冇有效訂閱」嘅結果,用嚟解鎖功能。

4. 兒童私隱

MathApp 係專登為小朋友設計嘅。因此:

  • 零廣告、零第三方追蹤。我哋冇裝 Crashlytics、Sentry、Firebase、Google Analytics 或者任何同類 SDK。App 亦冇要求 App 追蹤透明度(ATT)權限,因為我哋根本冇追蹤。
  • 小朋友介面冇外鏈、冇購買。所有設定、購買、報告、外部連結一律收喺 4 位數字家長密碼閘後面。
  • 我哋唔會主動向小朋友收集任何個人資料。小朋友個名係家長自己輸入,而且只留喺部機(除非家長自己開咗每日報告,寄去家長自己個信箱)。
  • 小朋友做題嘅內容同答案只留喺部機,除非家長自己開咗每日報告(報告會包括當日最多 5 條錯題同小朋友嘅答案,寄去家長自己個信箱,見 3.2)。

6. 保留幾耐

  • 部機上嘅資料:由你決定 —— 一直留到你刪除小朋友檔案或者刪走 app 為止。手寫草稿圖係例外,3 日後自動刪。
  • 匿名診斷紀錄:最多保留 12 個月,之後清走。
  • 每日報告:唔保留(見 3.2)。寄出咗嘅信件之後就住喺你自己個信箱,由你話事。

7. 家長有咩控制權

  • 停止匿名診斷:家長端 → 帳戶 → 匿名診斷 → 關(如果你之前開過)。連本機未傳嘅紀錄都會即刻清走。
  • 停止 email 報告:清空 email 欄位。
  • 刪除一個小朋友嘅全部資料:家長端 → 帳戶 → 刪除該檔案。會永久剷走佢嘅答題資料庫、遊戲存檔、設定同草稿。
  • 刪除全部資料:喺裝置上刪除 MathApp。
  • 查閱資料:你隨時喺「家長端 → 答題明細」睇齊所有紀錄,亦可以匯出一份文字檔帶走。
  • 已經傳出咗嘅匿名診斷紀錄入面冇任何識別到你或小朋友嘅資料,所以我哋冇辦法(亦冇必要)喺入面認返邊條係你嘅。

8. 資料安全

所有離開部機嘅傳輸都行 HTTPS 加密。家長密碼(PIN)嘅設計目的係擋住小朋友,唔係擋黑客 —— 佢只係存喺 app 自己嘅設定入面,請唔好當佢係保險箱。

9. 政策更新

如果我哋改咗呢份政策,會喺呢一頁更新內容同日期。重大改動(例如新增一條會傳出資料嘅通道)我哋會喺 app 內同時通知家長。

對私隱有疑問、想行使上述任何權利,請電郵 support@wisesagecreations.com,或者經支援頁聯絡我哋。

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective 22 September 2026 · Version 1.2. This English text is a translation of the Chinese version above; if the two ever differ, the Chinese version governs.

In one line: MathApp is a maths practice app for children. All learning data stays on your device by default. We show no ads, do no tracking, embed no third-party analytics SDKs, and never sell your data. Only two things ever leave the device, and a parent controls both: (1) anonymous diagnostics (off by default — a parent must tap “I agree” to turn it on) and (2) the daily email progress report (off until a parent enters their own email address).

1. Who runs this app

MathApp is built and operated by an independent developer. It is a maths practice app for the Hong Kong primary curriculum (Primary 1–6), running on iPhone and iPad. This policy explains how we handle your and your child's information. Questions? Reach us through the support page.

2. Data that stays on the device

The following is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded, and we cannot see it:

  • Your child's name and grade (entered by you; a nickname is fine)
  • All practice records: which questions, the answers given, right or wrong, time taken, hints used, difficulty level
  • Handwritten scratch images (automatically deleted after 3 days)
  • Parent settings and the 4-digit parent PIN
  • Game progress (pets, homestead, and so on)

MathApp requires no account, no sign-in, and has no cloud sync. Deleting a child profile in the app, or deleting the app, permanently removes this data from the device. We hold no backup of it.

3. Data that leaves the device (two channels)

3.1 Anonymous diagnostics (off by default; a parent must turn it on)

So that we can tell when the app crashes, freezes or fails to load questions on a child's device, the app can send anonymous technical records.

This is off by default. Until a parent goes to Parent area → Account → Anonymous diagnostics and taps “I agree”, the app records nothing and sends nothing — not even the install identifier described above is generated.

To turn on or off: Parent area → Account → Anonymous diagnostics. Before any choice is made you will see “I agree — turn on diagnostics” and “No thanks”; once on, you can switch it off at any time. Recording stops immediately, and any records still queued on the device are deleted right away.

These records travel via Google Apps Script into a Google Sheet that we control. They are used only to fix bugs and improve the app, never for advertising, and are not shared.

3.2 Daily email progress report (off by default; parent opt-in)

A parent may enter their own email address under Parent area → Account → Daily email report. Once set, on days your child practises, the app emails a summary to that address only.

  • Contents: child's name, grade, questions completed, accuracy, streak, weakest topic, up to 5 of the day's incorrect questions (with the child's answer and the correct one), and a 7-day comparison.
  • Path: app → a small program of ours on Google Apps Script → sent to your inbox via Gmail.
  • Is it stored? No. The program sends the message and writes neither your address nor the report to any database. The one exception is an anti-abuse counter that holds your address in memory for at most 6 hours before it expires.
  • To stop: clear the email field in the app. It stops immediately, with nothing left behind.

3.3 Question-bank updates (no personal data)

The app occasionally downloads updated question packs from Cloudflare file storage. This is an ordinary file download and carries no information about you or your child. As with any web request, the serving host sees your IP address in its technical logs.

3.4 Subscriptions and payment

If you subscribe, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase system. We never see your card number, Apple ID or payment details. All we receive from Apple is whether the device holds a valid subscription, which is what unlocks the paid features.

4. Children's privacy

  • No ads and no third-party tracking. We embed no Crashlytics, Sentry, Firebase, Google Analytics or comparable SDK. The app never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission because there is nothing to track.
  • The child-facing interface has no outbound links and no purchases. Settings, purchases, reports and external links all sit behind the 4-digit parent gate.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The child's name is entered by a parent and stays on the device, unless the parent has turned on the daily report that goes to the parent's own inbox.
  • The questions a child answers, and their answers, stay on the device, unless the parent has turned on the daily report (which includes up to 5 missed questions with the child's answers and goes to the parent's own inbox; see 3.2).

6. How long we keep things

  • On-device data: for as long as you keep it — until you delete the profile or the app. Handwritten scratch images are the exception and are deleted after 3 days.
  • Anonymous diagnostics: retained for at most 12 months, then deleted.
  • Daily reports: not retained by us (see 3.2). Once sent, the message lives in your own mailbox under your control.

7. Parental controls

  • Stop diagnostics: Parent area → Account → Anonymous diagnostics → off (if you turned it on). Queued local records are wiped immediately.
  • Stop email reports: clear the email field.
  • Delete one child's data: Parent area → Account → delete that profile. Their practice database, game save, settings and scratch images are permanently removed.
  • Delete everything: delete MathApp from the device.
  • Access your data: everything is visible under Parent area → Practice details, and can be exported as a text file.
  • Diagnostics already sent contain nothing that identifies you or your child, so we have no way (and no need) to single your records out from them.

8. Security

Everything that leaves the device travels over HTTPS. The parent PIN is designed to keep a child out, not an attacker — it is stored in the app's own settings, so please do not treat it as a vault.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and its date. For material changes — for example a new channel that sends data off the device — we will also notify parents inside the app.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise any of the rights above, please email support@wisesagecreations.com or reach us through the support page.
